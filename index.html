Support

Help

Getting Started

KUKA works fully offline — you don't need internet to fill out or submit a survey. You only need a connection to log in the first time, and to sync your data once you're back in range.

To record a survey:

1. Tap Home and choose a survey type (Mud Crab Survey or Coconut Crab Survey)
2. Fill in each section, tapping Next to move forward
3. Tap Submit Survey on the final Review step — your data is saved to this device immediately, even with no signal

Saving Your Work

Tap Save Draft at any point to save your progress without submitting — this is the only time your in-progress work is written to storage, so Records only ever shows drafts you deliberately chose to save. After saving, you'll be asked whether to keep editing, view Records, or go Home. Saved drafts appear in that survey's Records tab, badged "Draft," with a Continue button so you can pick up exactly where you left off. Submitted surveys stay safely on your device until you sync them — nothing is lost by being offline.

If you try to leave a survey partway through — using Home, the side menu, or your phone's back button — without saving first, KUKA will warn you and offer to save a draft before continuing, so you don't lose work by accident.

Syncing Your Data

Once you're back in network range:
1. Open the side menu and tap Synchronization
2. Tap Sync now to upload all unsynced surveys to your account
3. You can also export as CSV, Excel (.xlsx), or JSON at any time — CSV and JSON work fully offline; Excel needs a connection the first time to load, then works offline too. All give you a backup file you can transfer manually

Specimen Photos

When recording a crab in the Mud Crab or Biological survey, you can optionally attach a photo of the specimen. Photos are stored on your device and sync along with the rest of your survey data.

GPS Map Preview

After capturing a GPS location, a small map preview shows exactly where you are. Map imagery needs an internet connection to load — your coordinates are still saved and used normally if you're offline.

Language

Settings lets you change the app's menu and screen titles into several languages. Survey questions themselves are currently English-only.

Your Account

New users request access from the login screen; access is approved by your KUKA administrator. Once logged in, you'll stay logged in — even offline — until you tap Logout yourself. Your name and account details appear under Settings.

Records

Each survey type keeps its own Records tab, showing everything you've submitted on this device, whether it's synced yet or not.

Keeping the App Updated

Go to Settings → Check for Updates to make sure you're using the latest version of KUKA. This requires an internet connection.

Troubleshooting

App won't sync: Confirm you're online and logged in, then try again — already-synced records are automatically skipped, so it's always safe to retry.

Lost signal mid-survey: No problem — just keep working. Nothing needs a connection until you sync.

Accidentally deleted something: Deletions are permanent, so always export a backup from Synchronization before using Delete all local data in Settings.

Respondent Consent

The Socio-economic Survey collects personal details about the person you're interviewing. Before recording their information, explain the purpose of the survey and confirm they're willing to participate — then tick the consent checkbox in Section A. You won't be able to continue past that section without it.

Need More Help?

Contact your KUKA administrator or data manager for account access issues, questions about survey methodology, or anything not covered here.

About

Purpose

KUKA is a fisheries survey platform built for Papua New Guinea, designed to help surveyors collect Mud Crab and Coconut Crab data — including biological measurements and socio-economic information — quickly and reliably in the field.

Built for the Field

KUKA works fully offline, so surveyors can record complete surveys in remote coastal and island communities with no signal. Data stays safely on the device and syncs automatically once back in network range.

Data & Access

Survey data is stored securely and is only accessible to approved surveyors and administrators. New users are granted access by a KUKA administrator.

Privacy Notice

Some KUKA surveys collect personal information about respondents, including names, ages, and contact details. This information is collected only with the respondent's consent, and is used solely for fisheries research and management purposes.

Respondent data is stored securely and is only accessible to approved surveyors and administrators — it is never shared publicly or used for any purpose beyond this research. Surveyors are responsible for explaining the purpose of the survey and obtaining consent before recording respondent information.

Support

For questions about accessing KUKA, survey methodology, or reporting an issue, contact your KUKA administrator or data manager.
